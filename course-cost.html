The Cost of GUE Courses

An interactive estimate of instruction, registration and gas
Abstract Global Underwater Explorers (GUE) teaches a fixed curriculum, from its Fundamentals course to trimix, rebreather and cave diving. This page estimates what each course costs: a daily instruction rate times the course length, a one-time registration fee, and the breathing gas used on the course dives. Course lengths and minimum dives are those published by GUE. The gas is priced with the values set on The Cost of GUE Standard Gas Mixes. Tick courses to add up a training path. Your values are saved in this browser.
cost = d r + R + ∑i=1n ∑k gi,k(1) d = course days, r = instruction rate per day, R = registration fee, n = open-water dives, gi,k = cost of the gas in cylinder k on dive i (back gas, stages, diluent), priced as on the gas page: blend or premix, plus the fill fee
