Interactive figures on breathing-gas physics
Abstract Three interactive figures on the physics of breathing gas at depth: oxygen partial pressure and its exposure limits, the effect of pressure on gas volume, and a map of oxygen partial pressure over depth and oxygen fraction.

Figures

  • 2.1Oxygen partial pressure vs. depthPpO₂ for air, Nitrox 32 and a user-defined FO₂, with the 1.2 / 1.4 / 1.6 bar limits and resulting maximum operating depths.
  • 2.2Volume fraction vs. depthHow a gas volume shrinks with depth under Boyle’s law, with reference points at common training depths.
  • 2.3Isopleths of oxygen partial pressureInteractive map of pO₂ over depth and oxygen fraction, adjustable for altitude, temperature and surface pressure.
