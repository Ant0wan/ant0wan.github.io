The Cost of GUE Standard Gas Mixes

An interactive calculator from local component prices
Abstract Global Underwater Explorers (GUE) dive a short list of standard mixes. This calculator prices each mix from the cost of its components: helium, oxygen and air top-up, plus a fill fee for each cylinder. Where a shop sells a premix (Nitrox 32, Trimix 18/45), that price is shown as well. The default prices come from a Swiss fill station (see the sources). Change any value to match your local prices. Your values are saved in this browser.
