Notes on Infrastructure and Diving

Antoine Barthelemy
Abstract This site collects working notes from two fields. The first is a set of cheatsheets on Linux, DevOps and security, kept as short references for commands worth remembering. The second is a small set of interactive figures on the physics of breathing gas at depth: oxygen partial pressure, exposure limits and the effect of pressure on gas volume.
