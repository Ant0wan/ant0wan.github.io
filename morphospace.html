Raup’s Model of Shell Coiling

Three parameters, a whole morphospace of molluscan shells
Abstract A shell grows by moving a generating curve, the aperture, along a logarithmic spiral. Raup (1966) showed that three numbers are enough to reproduce most coiled shells: the whorl expansion rate W, the distance D of the aperture from the coiling axis, and the translation T along that axis. Change them below and compare with real shells.
