Isopleths of Oxygen Partial Pressure (pO2)

As a function of depth and oxygen fraction FO2
pO2 = FO2 × (Ph / P0 + D / 10.06)(1) Ph = P0 · e−Mgh/RT(2) M = 0.0289644 kg/mol  ·  g = 9.81 m/s²  ·  R = 8.314 J/(mol·K)  ·  10.06 m/atm
Hypoxic < 0.18 bar
Normoxic 0.18 ≤ pO₂ < 0.21
Hyperoxic 0.21 ≤ pO₂ < 1.40
Critical 1.40 ≤ pO₂ ≤ 1.60
Toxic > 1.60 bar

P0 sea-level reference: 1013.25 hPa Ph active surface: 1013.25 hPa T: 288.15 K
