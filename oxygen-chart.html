Isopleths of Oxygen Partial Pressure (pO2) as a function of Depth and Oxygen Fraction (FO2)
Hypoxic (< 1.0 bar)
Normoxic (1.0 - 1.4 bar)
Hyperoxic (1.4 - 1.6 bar)
Toxic (> 1.6 bar)
Partial Pressure: pO2 = FO2 × (Ph / P0 + D / 10.06)
P0 (Sea Level Ref): 1013.25 hPa
Ph (Active Surface): 1013.25 hPa
MSW/ATM: 10.06 m/atm
Barometric: Ph = P0 · exp[−(M·g·h) / (R·T)]
M (Air molar mass): 0.0289644 kg/mol
g (Gravity): 9.81 m/s²
R (Gas constant): 8.314 J/(mol·K)
T (Absolute Temp): 288.15 K
