The Periodic Table

The 118 elements, their properties and their discoverers
Abstract The elements arranged by atomic number Z, in eighteen groups and seven periods, with the lanthanides and actinides set below. Each cell gives the atomic number, the Pauling electronegativity, the symbol, the name, the standard atomic weight (or, in brackets, the mass number of the longest-lived isotope) and the outer electron configuration. Hover an element for everything else: its category, block, full configuration, first ionisation energy, oxidation states, melting and boiling points, density, state at 25 °C, and who discovered it, and when; click it to open its own page, where its occupied orbitals can be turned and explored. The table can be shaded by any of these properties, and rolled back in time to show only the elements known in a given year.
